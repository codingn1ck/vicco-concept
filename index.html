Before you try it.

Is this a fairness cream?

No. It doesn't lighten or whiten skin, and we'll never say it does. It's a haldi night cream for skin that looks clearer and more even over time.

Will it remove my pimple marks?

No cream can promise that, and you should be wary of any that does. What it gives you is a simple nightly step with turmeric and sandalwood oil. Everyone's skin responds differently.

Can I use it with my other products?

Use it as the last step at night. If you already use strong actives such as retinoids or acids, patch-test first and check with a dermatologist.

Is it the same Vicco my mother used?

Same haldi heritage, same family company. The tube, the routine and the way we talk about it are new.
